[h2]Configurable Options[/h2]
[h3]Normal Lightbugs[/h3]
[list]
[*] No feeding required
[*] Following Lightbugs travel with you between the surface and caves
[*] Invincible Lightbugs
[*] Adjustable Health (25–1000)
[*] Adjustable Health Regeneration (0%-50% Max HP per second)
[*] Adjustable Light Radius (1x–5x)
[*] Max Follow Limit (1–9)
[/list]
[h3]Wormwood's Lightbugs[/h3]
[list]
[*] Lifetime (1~100 Days)
[*] Invincible Lightbugs
[*] Adjustable Health (25–1000)
[*] Adjustable Health Regeneration (0%-50% Max HP per second)
[*] Adjustable Light Radius (1x–5x)
[*] Max Follow Limit (1–9)
[/list]
